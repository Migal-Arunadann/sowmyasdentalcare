# Dr. Sowmya's Dental Care — Website

Static multi-page website built strictly with **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript** for **Dr. Sowmya's Dental Care** (*No. 25/11, Cenotoph 2nd Ln, Austin Nagar, Teynampet, Chennai 600018*).

## Pages

1. **`index.html`** — Home overview, clinic highlights, interactive Before/After preview, Dr. Sowmya S. spotlight, verified Google reviews (`4.8 ★ · 139 reviews`), and `@drsowmyasdentalcare` Instagram Reels carousel.
2. **`about.html`** — Dr. Sowmya S. credentials (`BDS, Ragas Dental College & Hospital`), clinic philosophy, inclusivity badges (`Women-Owned`, `LGBTQ+ Friendly`), and full Clinic Tour gallery.
3. **`treatments.html`** — Preventive, Endodontic/Restorative, Implant/Surgical, and Cosmetic/Alignment care, interactive Before/After dental comparison slider, and Patient FAQs.
4. **`contact.html`** — Clinic contact details, click-to-call (`087545 16531`), WhatsApp direct link, opening hours (`Open · Closes 8:00 PM`), Google Plus Code (`26JW+JR Chennai, Tamil Nadu`), and embedded Google Map.

## Updating Live Content

- **Instagram Reels Carousel (`js/reels-data.js`)**: Paste public Reel links from `@drsowmyasdentalcare` (`https://www.instagram.com/reel/<CODE>/`) into `reelEmbedUrls` to display official Instagram Reels inside the horizontal carousel.
- **Before / After Slider Photos (`assets/`)**: Replace `assets/smile-before-demo.jpg` and `assets/smile-after-demo.jpg` with matching-angle patient photographs (4:3 aspect ratio recommended).
